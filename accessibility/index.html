Accessibility

I want everyone to be able to read this site, look through my work, and get in touch, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

The standard I follow

I aim for lyori.com to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. WCAG is the standard the U.S. Department of Justice points to for the Americans with Disabilities Act, and the one most accessibility laws around the world build on.

What I do

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus ring and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Pages use real headings, lists, landmarks, and labels so screen readers can move through them.
  • Images that carry meaning have text descriptions. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG contrast ratios, and the layout holds up at 320 pixels wide, at 200% zoom, and with larger text spacing.
  • Animation is kept short and plays once. If your device asks for reduced motion, the site turns it off.
  • The contact form has labels, announces errors, and does not use a CAPTCHA.

How I test

I check every page with automated tools (axe-core) and by hand: keyboard only, zoom and text resizing, narrow screens, and reduced motion. I test again whenever I add a page or change the design.

Known limits

  • Screenshots in the case studies show other products. Each has a text description, but the small text inside the images may not meet contrast rules. The case study text covers what they show.
  • Some links go to sites I don't control, like LinkedIn, GitHub, and Figma. Their accessibility is up to them.

Tell me about a barrier

If something on this site is hard to use, or you need any of it in another format, email me at hello@lyori.com. Tell me the page and what went wrong. I'll reply within 5 business days and work on a fix.
