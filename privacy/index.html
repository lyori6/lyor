Privacy

This is my personal site. I collect very little, I don't sell anything about you, and I'll delete what I have if you ask.

Last updated November 8, 2026.

What I collect

  • When you use the contact form: your name, your email address, and your message. I use them only to read and reply to your message.
  • When you browse: usage data through two analytics tools, described below. This includes the pages you visit, roughly where you are (country or city), your browser and device type, how you got here, and how you scroll and click.

I don't run ads, I don't have accounts or logins, and I don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Analytics

  • Google Analytics counts visits and shows me which pages people read. Google sets cookies to do this. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Microsoft Clarity shows me how people use the pages, through heatmaps and session recordings of scrolling, clicks, and mouse movement. Clarity masks what you type into the form. Microsoft collects this data with cookies and similar technology. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works the same without them.

Who helps run the site

  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Cloudflare runs the small service that delivers contact form messages to me.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's fonts, so your browser connects to Google to load them.

How long I keep it

I keep contact messages as long as I need them to reply and follow up, then delete them. Analytics data is kept according to each tool's settings, at most 14 months for Google Analytics and about 13 months for Clarity.

Your choices

You can ask me what I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email hello@lyori.com and I'll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, laws like the GDPR or California's privacy laws may give you more rights, and I'll honor them.

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If I change how the site handles your information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
